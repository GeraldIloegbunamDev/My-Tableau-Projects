Data Technician Bootcamp: BI & Data Storytelling (Tableau)

This repository contains interactive business intelligence dashboards and visual data models developed during Week 2 of the Data Technician Bootcamp. The project focuses on data storytelling, moving from raw datasets to public-facing analytical dashboards using Tableau Public and Tableau Desktop platforms.

🛠️ Technical Skillset & Chart Inventory

• Data Architecture Knowledge: Evaluated structural differences, security profiles, and connection protocols between Tableau Desktop, Cloud, Server, and Public.

• Geographic Visualizations: Developed density maps and filled map frameworks to locate demographic trends across regional boundaries.

• Hierarchical & Distribution Analytics: Constructed Tree Maps to represent relative data volumes and Pie Charts formatted with custom Percentage-of-Total table calculations.

• Trend & Metric Analysis: Formatted multi-series Line Charts utilizing Top-N parameters alongside Scatter Plots isolating continuous measure correlations.

• Interactive Frameworks: Implemented global parameters, dashboard cross-filters, contextual tooltips, and discrete Single Value dropdown selectors.

📂 Project Summaries & Deliverables


🗺️ 1. GB Employment & Industry Analytics Dashboard

• Live Dashboard Link: https://public.tableau.com/app/profile/gerald.iloegbunam/vizzes
• Overview: A comprehensive labor market diagnostic dashboard examining economic shifting across the United Kingdom. It cross-references raw industrial categories against geographic locations to display macro hiring dynamics.

Dashboard Component Breakdown

+------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+

|                               GLOBAL HEALTH INSIGHTS                               |
+----------------------------------------+-------------------------------------------+

| Top 10 Industry (Bar Chart)            | Change by Industry (Diverging Bar)        |
| - Tallies absolute workforce volumes.  | - Tracks positive vs. negative shifting.  |
+----------------------------------------+-------------------------------------------+

| Average % Change by City (Map)         | Industry Distribution (Tree Map)          |
| - Plots regional regional change.      | - Segments workforce sizes visually.      |
+----------------------------------------+-------------------------------------------+
• Top 10 Industry Chart: An absolute Bar Chart isolating macro-employment distributions. It reveals Administrative and Support Service Activities as the dominant workforce anchor, scaling past 120,000 active roles.
• Change by Industry Visualization: A sorted, dual-directional Bar Chart tracking growth metrics. It exposes contraction patterns across old manufacturing and public utility fields against major growth in service spaces.
• Average % Change by City Map: A localized geographic coordinate visualization mapping regional shifts. It reveals distinct employment clustering, highlighting localized urban changes across the UK mainland.
• Industries Tree Map: A nested proportional volume map displaying sector layout. It color-codes structural business distributions like Real estate activities and Human health activities for immediate layout comparison.

🌍 2. Global Health & Macro Trends (Gapminder Model)

• Overview: A global health monitoring platform utilizing multi-national datasets to track continuous biometric outcomes over extensive chronological periods.
• Life Expectancy by Continent: A sorted categorical analysis mapping macro baseline longevity metrics across universal global coordinates.
• Life Expectancy Trend Line Chart: A multi-series, color-indexed timeline plot utilizing Top-5 parameter caps to isolate peak historical health vectors over time.
• Population by Gender Pie Chart: A dynamic, filter-driven proportional model tracking micro-demographic splits utilizing reactive percentage calculations.
• Life Expectancy vs. BMI Scatter Plot: A multi-axis coordinate map plotting continuous health outcomes against baseline body metrics, utilizing continent color overlays to expose geographic health discrepancies.

🎵 3. Spotify Feature Distribution Analysis

• Live Dashboard Link: View on Tableau Public
• Overview: An exploratory data analysis (EDA) model parsing macro audio tracking characteristics (including popularity, valence, energy, tempo, and acousticness profiles) across diverse musical spaces to identify high-viability audience targets.
